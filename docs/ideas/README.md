They are not work items and do not satisfy Definition of Ready.

Terminal records remain in this index with their rationale and links.

Create future records from the [idea template](../../templates/idea.md). Promote
an idea only by creating and linking the appropriate `EPIC`, `FEAT`, `TASK`, or
`DEC` record; promotion does not bypass the normal delivery gates.
